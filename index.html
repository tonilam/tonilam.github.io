Portfolio

Squizify

Squizify is a Software as a Service (SaaS) application dedicated to providing digital food safety and compliance solutions primarily for the hospitality and food service industries. The platform replaces traditional paper-based food safety management systems (FSMS) with a modern, cloud-based workflow to ensure businesses meet regulatory standards, such as those governed by HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points) principles.

The core function of Squizify is to provide a complete operational control system that enables users to digitize all food safety records, temperature checks, and compliance documentation on a single platform. This system includes features for real-time monitoring, automated alerts for failed checks, incident reporting, and data-driven insights, which allow managers to easily demonstrate audit-readiness and maintain high hygiene standards across multiple locations, ultimately reducing compliance risks and improving operational efficiency.

Urpla

Urpla was a Software as a Service (SaaS) application designed specifically to streamline and simplify the town planning development application (DA) process for both the planning industry and local councils in Australia.

The platform provided an all-in-one, end-to-end digital service by automating and standardizing several complex steps. Its core functionality included a GIS web service to identify sites and intersecting planning overlays, a rules engine to determine required assessments based on user inputs, and services for automated document generation (Word & PDF) and data collection from government sources. This solution was notably successful in reducing the average time required for the town planning process by an estimated 75%–90% (saving up to 40 hours) by minimizing manual data entry and creating a single, standardized source of truth for all development application data.

OpenCMS integrations

The OpenCMS Regional Deployment Project successfully established a centralized, enterprise-level Content Management System (CMS) solution for a major global financial services client, similar to Prudential, managing their public-facing websites across five key Asian markets: Hong Kong, Vietnam, South Korea, Singapore, and Malaysia. This initiative focused on architecting and deploying a single OpenCMS instance that leveraged its native multi-site and multilingual capabilities to standardize brand identity and user experience across the regions. By moving away from fragmented local sites, the project achieved unified technical standards and established a foundation for consistent content delivery and streamlined maintenance.

A core achievement of the project was the implementation of robust workflow and governance controls critical for the financial sector. Custom content approval processes were integrated within OpenCMS to ensure strict compliance with local regulatory and legal requirements before content was published in each regional market. Furthermore, the deployment required configuring a highly scalable Java-based environment to guarantee optimal performance and high availability, supporting significant traffic loads while simultaneously managing complex technical integrations with the client's existing identity and analytics platforms. This ensured the solution was not only a technical success but also a powerful business enablement tool for the client's regional marketing and compliance teams.
